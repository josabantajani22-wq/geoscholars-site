Geology vacancy FAQs

Which exams recruit geologists in India?

UPSC Combined Geo-Scientist (for GSI Geologist/Geophysicist/Chemist and CGWB Scientist-B), ONGC Graduate Trainee CBT, GATE-based PSU recruitment (NMDC, Coal India, Oil India, ONGC), state PSC Assistant Geologist exams (OPSC, JPSC, CGPSC, MPPSC, RPSC and others) and departmental recruitments in MECL, HCL, UCIL and AMD.

What is the eligibility for a geologist vacancy?

Most central and PSU posts need an M.Sc. / M.Sc. (Tech) in Geology, Applied Geology, Geophysics or an allied subject; some state posts and JRF positions accept B.Sc. (Hons.) Geology. Age limits are usually 21–32 with category relaxations — check each card above.
